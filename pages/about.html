I’m an information systems student at BYU, a lifelong learner, and someone who loves bringing people and technology together to solve useful problems.

01 / MY STORY

People first.
Purpose always.

I grew up in rural Utah and first fell in love with programming by making things that could help someone. Later, serving a mission introduced me to people and perspectives from different backgrounds. That experience helped me see how much I enjoy listening, learning, and working alongside others.

Information systems feels like a natural meeting point for those interests. I get to understand a real need, think through the process behind it, and help shape technology that makes the experience better for people.

I’m especially curious about product management and the possibility of teaching information systems in the future.
